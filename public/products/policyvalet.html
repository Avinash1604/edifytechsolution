Questions, answered.

Still curious? Write to us using the details at the bottom of the page.

Is PolicyValet an insurance company?

No. PolicyValet doesn't sell insurance. It is an organiser for the policies you already hold with any insurer, so you and your family always know what cover you have.

Which kinds of policies can I add?

Health, life, motor, home, travel and term policies, plus an Other category for anything else, such as gadget or pet cover.

Who can see my policies?

Only you, until you invite a family member and choose which policies they can access. You can change or revoke that access whenever you like.

What if a family member hasn't joined yet?

Their invitation shows as pending. You'll get a reminder if it isn't accepted, and you can resend or cancel it at any time.

Will I be reminded before a policy expires?

Yes. Renewals due soon appear on your home screen with a countdown, and you'll receive alerts for renewals and premium due dates.
